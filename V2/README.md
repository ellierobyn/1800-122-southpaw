# 1800-122 Southpaw V2
This version of the board is the actively in development version using local repos and generally better best practices.

NOTE: The footprints used in the 122-LIB "SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_1.00u_**PnP**" & "SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_Plated_1.00u_**PnP**" are forked from the official KiCad key switch repo. Positionally they are the same to "SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_1.00u" & "SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_Plated_1.00u" **EXCEPT** their component origin is located at the centroid of the physical hotswap socket so the manufacturing files exported are compatible with Pick and Place machines for automated PCB assembly. The 2 locating pin holes also had their nominal sizes shrunk by .001" to accommodate JLCPCB's capabilities.
